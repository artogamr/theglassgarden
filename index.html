Small worlds, made by hand

The Glass Garden creates terrariums that feel like a window into somewhere else: a mossy woodland with a deer at the edge, a fairy cottage by a stream of blue pebbles, a golden glass lantern of succulents.

Every piece is built by hand layer by layer, so no two are exactly alike. They suit a desk, a shelf, a windowsill, or a gift for someone who has everything.
