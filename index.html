What is a closed terrarium?

A small garden sealed in glass that mostly looks after itself.

A garden that recycles

The sealed glass reuses its own water and air.

How it works

Moisture rises, settles on the glass and trickles back down to water the plants again.

Built in layers

Pebbles for drainage, then soil, moss and tropical plants, all working together.

Why people love them

Very little upkeep, and a piece of nature on your desk or shelf.

Caring for your terrarium

A few simple habits keep your little garden green and happy.

Light

Bright, indirect sunlight only. Keep it out of direct sun.

Water

Water sparingly and avoid overwatering. Mist only when there is no condensation and the soil is dry. Use RO or distilled water.

Fog on the glass

Lots of condensation? Open the lid for a few hours. If it is heavily foggy, leave it open for 2 to 3 hours.

Temperature

18°C to 28°C. Keep it away from AC vents and heat.

Tidying up

Remove dead and fallen leaves, trim yellow ones and keep the glass clean. Don’t open it often. No fertilizer for 6 to 8 months.

Where to place it

Keep it indoors, away from direct sun and AC vents. Avoid dark corners.
